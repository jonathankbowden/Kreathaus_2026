Strategic Partnerships

How do you bring travel to where people already are?

Role Principal Design Leader
Timeline 2024 – Present
Platform Cross-Platform

Overview

Travel planning happens everywhere—in conversations with AI assistants, while reading articles, browsing social media. I lead design for Expedia's strategic partnerships, embedding travel experiences into the platforms millions already use daily.

OpenAI / ChatGPT Amazon Alexa+ Perplexity Lenovo Condé Nast

ChatGPT Integration

We designed the experience for connecting your Expedia account to ChatGPT—letting the AI understand your travel preferences, past trips, and loyalty status. The result: personalized recommendations that feel like they come from a friend who knows exactly how you like to travel.

Deep Personalization

Beyond basic preferences, we explored how connected accounts could create truly personalized experiences. Imagine trip recommendations based on your Spotify playlists, Netflix watch history, or Instagram saves—travel that reflects who you actually are.

Amazon Alexa+

Voice-first travel planning through Alexa+. We designed conversational flows that let travelers research, compare, and book trips entirely through voice—perfect for hands-free planning at home.

"Take the Trip"—a seamless integration that turns Condé Nast Traveler editorial content into bookable experiences. Read about a destination, then book it without leaving the article. Content becomes commerce.

Perplexity AI

Bringing real-time travel inventory into AI search. When someone asks Perplexity about flights to Tokyo, they get actual availability and prices—not just information, but actionable options.

Philosophy

Meet travelers where they already are.

Rather than forcing people to come to Expedia, we're embedding travel planning into the tools and platforms they use every day. Each partnership extends our reach while creating genuinely useful experiences for travelers.
