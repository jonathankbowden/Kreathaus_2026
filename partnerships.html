Overview

Travel planning happens everywhere—in conversations with AI assistants, while reading articles, browsing social media. I lead design for Expedia's strategic partnerships, embedding travel experiences into the platforms millions already use daily. Two pillars guide the work: Discovery and Creation on partner platforms, and deep Platform Integration.

Condé Nast Traveler

What if you combined the most trusted source of travel inspiration with the AI of the most trusted booking platform? We designed "Take the Trip"—a seamless integration turning Condé Nast Traveler articles into bookable experiences. Readers move from an existing article to Make It Yours to a fully curated trip without ever leaving the content.

OpenAI / ChatGPT

As humanity begins to test how much to trust AI, how might connected accounts bring unexpected delight? We designed the experience for connecting your Expedia account to ChatGPT—letting the AI understand your travel preferences, past trips, and loyalty status. A few weeks later: "Thought you might like..." becomes "A trip just for you."

OpenAI — Connected accounts integration

Tesco Loyalty

What if you could turn grocery shopping points into the Paris dream trip, tailored to your shopping history? We designed the Tesco Clubcard to OneKey points exchange—an offer surfaces in the Tesco App, exchanged in the Hotels.com App, and personalized trip planning begins.

Amazon Alexa+

When to save? When to explore more? When to book? When to suggest? Voice-first travel planning through Alexa+ required designing conversational flows that balance helpfulness with restraint—knowing when to prompt and when to listen.

Amazon Alexa+ — Conversational travel planning

Philosophy

Meet travelers where they already are.

Rather than forcing people to come to Expedia, we're embedding travel planning into the tools and platforms they use every day. Each partnership extends our reach while creating genuinely useful experiences for travelers.
