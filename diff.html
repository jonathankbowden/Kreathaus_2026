01 — ABOUT

I build things people trust

I'm Jonathan Bowden — a Principal Design Leader who's spent his career turning ambiguous, high-stakes problems into products millions of people rely on.

At Expedia Group I've led the design of the creator economy platform from zero, brought travel to AI partners like ChatGPT, Alexa+, and Perplexity, and pioneered AI-powered search before it was mainstream. Before that I cut my teeth at agencies and startups where scrappiness was the only option.

I'm at my best when the brief is "figure it out" — when there's no playbook, no precedent, and the only path forward is one you design yourself.

Advice I'd give to designers

Learn to build. Whether it's code, prototypes, or side projects — the closer you are to the material, the better your design gets.

Say yes to the ambiguous projects. The ones with no brief and no precedent are where you grow the fastest.

Design the relationship, not just the interface. Trust compounds. Quick wins don't.
