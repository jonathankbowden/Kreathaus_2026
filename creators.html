Overview

In 2022, we started with a simple question:

How do you connect to travelers,
through the most trusted voices?

This trusted voice part is crucial, and also tricky, as it's different for everyone. If you're a big basketball fan, visiting Indiana, you may look to Caitlin Clark for recommendations... or for you, the trusted voice may be a musician, an actor, a scholar, or an influencer.

The Swarm

We kicked off with what we call "The Swarm"—a cross-functional deep dive bringing together product, engineering, data science, and design to map the creator landscape.

The goal: understand where creator content could most meaningfully influence traveler decisions, and identify the highest-leverage opportunities to build around.

Begin with Solid Tooling

Coming out of the swarm, we knew we needed to drastically uplevel the foundational tooling. We started with a clear and concise first-mile onboarding experience.

Embedded Tools on Platform

We built tools utilizing single sign on for creators to quickly access proprietary data and insights, as well as links, directly inside of Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo.

Show Them the Money

We overhauled the performance dashboard, with both past performance, as well as insights and suggestions.

Vision – An Expedia Storefront for All

How might we provide creators with more than just links and insights? What if we provided their own stores on Expedia, fully customizable with their own flair, with collections, videos, curated trips and more?

Travel Shops v1

We shipped the first version in under six months. V1 focused on the essentials — a customizable storefront, curated collections, and affiliate links — giving creators a real presence on Expedia for the first time.

Travel Shops — What's Next

With v1 validated, we expanded the vision. Richer customization, video integration, and AI-powered recommendations are turning Travel Shops into dynamic storefronts that evolve with each creator's audience.

Curation Across the Platform

Where might creator content be most valuable?

Deep Collaboration

How to tackle such a huge problem? We gathered the cross-functional team of designers, strategists, engineers, marketers, stakeholders and more, to deep dive into the current experience, and then dream up what could be.

Vision Video

We crafted a vision video to align leadership and rally the broader organization. It painted a picture of what creator-powered travel could become — not just a product pitch, but a north star for the team.

Shopping

Curated Trips

Curated Trips let creators bundle flights, hotels, and activities into bookable itineraries. Travelers get a trusted recommendation; creators get a seamless way to monetize their expertise.

Curation Throughout Shopping

Creator content doesn't live in a silo. We wove it into search results, property pages, and checkout — surfacing trusted recommendations at the moments travelers are actually making decisions.

Creator Tooling

Creator Hub

The Creator Hub centralizes everything — analytics, content management, link generation, and payouts. One login, one dashboard, built to scale from micro-influencers to major media partners.

Content & Analytics

Real-time performance data gives creators visibility into what's working. Click-through rates, booking conversions, and earnings breakdowns help them refine their content strategy over time.
