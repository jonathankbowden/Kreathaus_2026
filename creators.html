Creators

How do you connect travelers to the most trusted voices in travel?

Role Principal Design Leader
Timeline 2022–Present
Platform Web + Mobile

Overview

The creator economy was transforming how people discover everything—except travel. I led the design of Expedia's creator platform from zero, building tools that let travel creators monetize their influence while giving travelers authentic recommendations from voices they trust.

0→1 First in Industry
2k+ Travel Shops Launched
2x Conversion Rate
3x Save Rate

Design Swarm

We pioneered a "Design Swarm" methodology—intensive collaborative sprints that brought together diverse perspectives rapidly. The 2022 swarm: 2 weeks, 24 participants, 7 specialties, 3 continents. By 2024: 3 weeks, 32 participants, 8 specialties, 5 continents.

Creator Toolbox

We built a comprehensive suite of tools for creators: onboarding flows, content management, performance analytics, and monetization dashboards. Every feature was designed to reduce friction and help creators focus on what they do best—inspiring travel.

Travel Shops

Travel Shops became the centerpiece—personalized storefronts where creators curate hotels, experiences, and destinations for their audiences. Each shop reflects the creator's unique perspective and aesthetic.

AI-Assisted Creation

We integrated Romie AI to help creators generate content faster—from trip descriptions to curated guides. The AI learned each creator's voice, ensuring authenticity while dramatically reducing the time to publish.

Performance Analytics

Creators needed to understand their impact. We designed analytics dashboards showing audience insights, booking attribution, and revenue performance—giving creators the data they need to grow their business.

Evolution

The platform evolved from serving travel bloggers to social-first creators on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. Each iteration expanded reach while maintaining the core promise: authentic travel inspiration that converts.
