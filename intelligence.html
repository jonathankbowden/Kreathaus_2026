Intelligence

How do you introduce a new way of exploring, before anyone had ever heard of "ai"?

Role Principal Design Leader
Timeline 2021 – 2022
Platform Native + Web
Add image: intelligence-hero.jpg

Overview

In 2021, before ChatGPT launched and AI became mainstream, I led the design of Expedia's first AI-powered search experience. The challenge: reimagine how travelers discover and plan trips through conversational interfaces, without the benefit of established patterns or user expectations.

Approach

We used a dual-track approach—balancing near-term implementation with long-term discovery. This allowed us to ship incremental improvements while exploring bold new interaction models.

Add image: intelligence-02.jpg

Voice Interactions

We explored conversational voice interfaces that let travelers search naturally—"Find me a beach hotel in Cancun for my anniversary." This work laid the groundwork for how Expedia would later integrate with voice assistants.

Add image: intelligence-03.jpg

Tailored Discovery

Rather than showing generic results, we designed personalized guides that adapted to each traveler's preferences, past trips, and current context. The experience felt like having a knowledgeable friend who knew exactly what you'd love.

Dark Mode

As part of our systematic design evolution, we introduced dark mode across the experience—improving accessibility and creating a more immersive browsing experience for late-night trip planning.

Add image: intelligence-07.jpg

Outcome

This work is now the foundation of AI experiences across Expedia today.

What started as early exploration became the design language and technical foundation for Expedia's current AI features. The patterns we established—conversational search, personalized recommendations, contextual understanding—are now core to how millions of travelers plan their trips.
