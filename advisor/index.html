How to export from Empower
  1. Sign in to the Empower Personal Dashboard on the web.
  2. Open Banking → Transactions (or Accounts → All transactions).
  3. Set the date range you want — a year or more gives the advisor the best picture.
  4. Click the CSV download icon above the transaction list.
  5. Drop the downloaded transactions.csv here. Mint, Monarch, and most bank CSVs work too.
